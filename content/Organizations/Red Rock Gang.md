# Summary
The Red Rock Gang is a crime organization build in [Peppercorn City](Peppercorn%20City.md). They are focused around red rock, which is a token they use to identify each other. When Shnarkle offended several of their members, he was robbed. Afterwards Murbrek and Shnarkle worked together to kill two of their guards and get Shnarkle's stuff back. In their escape, they saw a baby red dragon under the control of the leader, suggesting more powers at their disposal.
