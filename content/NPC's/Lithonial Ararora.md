# Summary
Lithonial is the current mayor of the city of Ouromael. **Lithonial** is a good mayor, often helping the city in times of need. She had managed to defeat some great devil long ago, something with relations to goats, as she has banned their entering of Ouromael.



# Relationships
## Locations:

Lithonial is the mayor of Ouromael, and has a love for the people and the town.
