# Summary
Sarkas is an elf that resides in [Dragonview](Locations/Dragonview). He is often described as having a piercing gaze, and fills rooms with his overbearing presence. He was the person who gathered together the {Party Name}, and explained the events at [Aldritch-Farm](Aldritch's%20Farm.md). During the events of [Session 3](Session-3-notes), he left for one of the [three major cities](Locations/index#larger-cities) of Paleros.
The Party later came across him in Session 5, in the city Ouromael. Here he found it important to find a specific map or item, that would lead to an important artifact. He claimed that his desire to locate this map was only out of curiosity, and not on material wealth.
# Description
Sarkas has black hair, and stands very tall for an elf, and has pearly white skin. He has a very overbearing personality, seeming to fill the room with his presence
# Relationships
## Locations:
### Dragonview
Sarkas has a deep love for Dragonview, as shown by his focus on trying to save it by hiring the party to stop the events in Session 1.
### Ouromael
Sarkas traveled to Ouromael at some point during the events of Session 3. He is well known around Ouromael.
## NPC's:
### Lord Leobardus
Sarkas seems to have a high opinion of Leobardus. They originally met when the adventurer was in his mid thirties, approximately 40 years before the current time period. Sarkas had sought him out of curiosity, as it interested him to meet a human adventurer who had already accomplished much in his little time of life.
## PC's:
### Shnarkle
During the events of Session 1, Sarkas wished to remain anonymous. However, Snarkle had managed to pester the name out of the elf.
