# Description
Surprisingly short even for a dwarf, Groster Platehealm has a very lighthearted nature. He is very talkative, which makes for an interesting contrast to the average dwarfs gruff attitude.

According to the captains rumors, Leobardus had left with his wife, appearing to be very focused and planning to leave for a mission or adventure.

Groster believes that Lithonial Ararora seems to be jealous of Leobardus, but has sent many search parties after Leobardus.
