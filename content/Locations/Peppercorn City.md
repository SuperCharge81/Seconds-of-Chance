# Description
It has a circular wall around it, with several tiers. The lower class has been built around the outside ring, along the edge of the wall. The middle class gathers around the central ring and the upper class lives in the upper ring.

# Summary
Peppercorn City was named because the discoverer saw a peppercorn when entering the area, an thus named it as such. It was the year 550 was when it was founded, and has grown steadily for the past 200 years. Is is now a bustling hub of a city. At some point in the past few years, a dragon had been terrorizing the city, which was somehow stopped by [Lord Leobardus](Lord%20Leobardus). The upper class in Peppercorn City are notorious for their long, strange names.

# Relationships
## NPC's:
### Lord Leobardus
Lord Leobardus is a very popular figure in Peppercorn City because of him defeating a dragon that had been terrorizing the area. He visits from time to time.

### House Marshal
Lord & Lady Marshal are an upper class couple in Peppercorn city. They are not particularly high in the social structure, but still have influence over the city

### Lord Farmer
Lord Farmer is a very important person in the city, but is well known for being very reclusive, rarely speaking with anyone.
