# Description
Dragonview is a very small town standing between Ouromael and Peppercorn City. It stands against the Peppercorn Forest. It consists of mostly humans, but also has gnomes, elves, and a few dwarves.

# Summary
Dragonview is the location where the Campain was originally started. This is where [Sarkas](NPC's/Sarkas) originally gathered a group of adventurers to investigate a set of interesting circumstances in the north-western reaches of the Peppercorn Forest. It seemed that some dog-like creatures had taken all of [Aldritch's](Aldritch) sheep, and taken over his farm. Upon further investigation, it was discovered that it was [Gardonin](Gardonin), inside the dead forest, who was commanding a tribe of Gnolls. The events of [Aldritch's Farm](Aldritch's%20Farm.md) were likely the most excitement that this town has had in decades
