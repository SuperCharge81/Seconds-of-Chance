# Description
### Before Gardonins' Banishment
The Ruined Village is located inside the Dead forest. Before Gardonins' banishment, it was characterized with dark twisted trees, strange mists, and eerie noises. This village is made up of strange stone structures, that have obviously been falling apart for hundreds of years. There is also a large pillar in the center of the town, seemingly made up of several stone blocks placed on top of each other. Each structure is covered with a brownish green ooze, and any wooden items have long been rotted away. Straight thought the center of the village is a large trench or ravine that continues for miles., inside which is [[Gardonins Layer]].
# Summary
The ruined village is the place where [Gardonin](Gardonin.md) had his layer built underneath. Gardonins curses were likely what was causing the strange occurrencest surrounding the [Dead Forest](Dead%20Forest.md).
