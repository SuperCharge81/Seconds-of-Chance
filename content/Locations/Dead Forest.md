# Description
Before Gardonins' banishment, the Dead Forest was characterized with dark twisted trees, strange mists, and eerie noises. There were reports of several ghosts wandering its depths, and mist fills the air. The trees look horrible, as if they have been cursed into existence, with odd protrusions that look like dead faces sticking out. The trees also seem to be planted in strange, mirror images of each other. Animals found within also seem to have the same disturbing curse.
# Summary
Supposedly around the time that Gardonin had set up his layer in the [Ruined Village](Ruined%20Village.md), the Dead Forest began to have its strange curse. However, the origin and make of this curse is unknown. Following the events of Session 3, the Forests curse seems to have left, as the eerie feeling left, and everything seemed more healthy.
