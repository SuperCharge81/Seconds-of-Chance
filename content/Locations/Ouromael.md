# Description
Ouromael is a great elvish city, with a large Elvish population, and is reflected in its architecture. Standing in the center of the city is the tree of the great light. It is made of blue white energy, and is absolutely beautiful.

# Summary
Ouromael is one of the three major cities of Paleros, and has a predominantly elven population. This is the retirement place of [Leobardus](Leobardus.md) and so, upon traveling there, we discovered his disappearance.

# Relationships
