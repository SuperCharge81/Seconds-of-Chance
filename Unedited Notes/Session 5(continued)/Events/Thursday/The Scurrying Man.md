Event Name: Shnarkle search for Leobardus

>[!Summary]
>Based while the party had split apart and was gathering information

Shnarkle searched around gnome-town inside Ouromael. He found a crazy gnome to find answers. He shares a crazy story about a small man who seems to be following him all the time, but no one will believe him. He shares that the little scurrying man had stolen Leobardus, which he of course learned in a dream. He then goes on to deeply explain that this little man is a shapeshifting dragonborn. Shnarkle continues to act as though he completely believes the strange man, going so far as to buy him several drinks, as well as leaving a note after Valver's eventual drunken slumber. After leaving the tavern they were talking in, Shnarkle is startled to see a small shape that he believes for a split second to be the little Scurrying man. He is slightly embarrassed to find that is simply a cat.

NPC Name: Valver Fluketrick
Location: Gnome-town

Shnarkle proceeds to share his story, which we all find to be hilarious.
