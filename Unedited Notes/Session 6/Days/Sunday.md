Summary: We start traveling to peppercorn, stopping at dragon view for Saturday night.

>Peppercorn city was named because the discoverer saw a peppercorn when entering the area, an thus named it as such. The year 550 was when it was founded, and has grown for the past 200 years. Is is now a bustling hub of a city. it has a circular wall around it, with tiers, with the lower class around the outside ring. Middle class gathers around the central ring. the upper class lives in the upper ring, and are notorious for their long, strange names.

A guard stops us at the gate, suspicious that we are stopping at such an unusual time. Upon discovering that Lilith is in our party, the guard quickly stops harassing us and invites us in. Sharkel asks for a gnome town, but is disappointed to find that the majority of the town is human.
Upon reaching the tavern, there is a lot of attention on Lilith, because Leobardus is her father. We learn that Leobardus had saved peppercorn from some dragon which was terrorizing the town.

Shortly after we arrive and get settled at the Drowsy Dragon, a messenger walks over and hands a letter. She notices that many of the guests are discretely watching, so she moves to her rooms to have more privacy. After about ten minutes, she hears a knock and a second letter is slid under her door.
The first letter contains a invitation to Lord and Lady Marshals ball. The Marshals are average in the upper classes. Not particularly high royalty, but also important enought to have influence.
The second letter contains a similar invitation to Lord Farmer's ball, at the same time as the Marshals ball. Lord Farmer is rather reclusive, but hightly influential in Peppercorn.

---

Shnarkel attempts to bully the bartender into getting him some juice. However the bartender has gotten annoyed to the point of getting one of his jumpers to kick Shnarkel out. Upon seeing this, Murbrek tries to stop the jumper by standing in front and intimidating. However, both get stopped by Lilith, who hears the curses of Shnarkel, and comes downstairs. She apologizes to the barkeep, before she arranges that he will go somewhere else to find juice and bed. Sharkel promptly wanders off and falls asleep on the side of the road.

Lilith continues a conversation with the barkeep, asking about the political climate of the city. He talks about how Lord and Lady Leobardus had just passed through, and left a few days ago. He mentions that Lord Farmer is likely to know Leobardus's intended destination, but is notorious for being difficult to talk to. The Barkeep mentioned that Lord Stone spent a lot of time with her father, so she might have the best luck talking to him.

Lilith, Rar, and Mubrek discuss what to do next. It is decided that Mubrek will find Shnarkel and babysit, while Lilith and Rar gather clothes for Lord Farmer's Ball.

___

Murbrek eventually finds Shnarkle lying drunk and robbed in an ally. He splashes him with water, then brings him into the woody wood chuck diner and gets him a giant glass of grape juice. He then forces him to bed, then Shnarkle falls asleep immediately.
